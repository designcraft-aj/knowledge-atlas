About Knowledge Atlas

Knowledge Atlas is a living, personal map of the artists, authors, and philosophers I keep returning to — placed where they lived, and shown across the span of when they were alive.

Hover a portrait to see who they are and the work or idea that stayed with me. Drag the timeline to move through history, or switch to “All eras” to see everyone at once.
